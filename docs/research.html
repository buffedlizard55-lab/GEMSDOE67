The hypothesis

Jointly learning undirected orientation and tip/junction likelihood may preserve weak fault continuations that isolated pixel classification loses. Lithologic contacts, drainage alignments and fan margins can mimic the signal.

The prototype uses a small shared-trunk model, not Batra’s full spatial CNN. Original catalogue polylines were unavailable; skeleton/PCA labels are proxies. It is not evidence that a particular vent or permeable fault has been discovered.
