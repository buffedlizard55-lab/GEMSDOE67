The five candidate geological hypotheses this session produced, ranked, and what happened to each, live in docs/research/hypotheses.md (rendered below verbatim in structure). The lane definition, the negative results and the format forensics are in the same folder.

H67-A — multi-physics evidence + context head + greedy cover shipped

19 provided bands → per-family structure-tensor lineament evidence, axial orientation (cos 2θ, sin 2θ), multi-physics axial order parameter, multi-scale context. Supervised head; emission by greedy kernel-cover. Worth ≈ +0.05 AUC for context alone. Pooled holdout DTI 0.269.

H67-B — public USGS-derived LiDAR scarp and radiometric families no measurable gain

13 band-level evidence sources from lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif (USGS 3DEP 1 m lidar), geodawn_rad_u8.tif and geodawn_extensions_u8.tif (GeoDAWN, DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ). Pooled DTI 0.2691 versus 0.2689 without them — i.e. indistinguishable; kept for the tip-zone argument the instrument cannot test, reported as a negative result for the score claim.

H67-C — full-resolution 1 m DEM scarplet chains blocked here

Needs the competition's 1m_DEM_links.csv tiles from USGS 3DEP (free, official); the tiles are not reachable from this sandbox and exceed its free disk. Source named on the sources page.

H67-D — raw ComCat seismicity lineaments blocked here

The provided seismic bands use a 100 km radius and cannot resolve sub-kilometre lineaments. The raw ANSS ComCat catalogue is free and official but not reachable from this sandbox.

H67-E — thermal-spring corroboration (GDR 1391) deferred

Well/spring temperature and chemistry points (DOI 10.15121/1881483, CC BY 4.0) already mirrored in this family of repositories. Deferred: it needs its own masked-holdout test, and the session budget is three experiments. Leakage warning: drop the dist_known_fault_px column, it is label-derived.

Negative results are deliverables

  • The lane's geometry gate (sparse, topologically clean traces) scores 0.1149 versus 0.2689 for the same head emitting a greedy cover — a measured argument that geometry-aware decoding, left to act as a mass filter, loses more coverage than it gains in precision under α = 0.2, β = 0.8.
  • Carrying calibrated probabilities as the raster values scores 0.0034 where the same sites with binary 1.0 score 0.1149: the metric is linear in single-pixel mass, so a binary field dominates.
  • The incumbent family (dots on the dilated mapped faults) scores 0.0014 on a blocked holdout: hidden faults are not simply the neighbourhood of mapped ones.
  • The public LiDAR/radiometric families add ≈ nothing to the pooled holdout DTI (+0.0002).
