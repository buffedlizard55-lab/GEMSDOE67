3. What this lane is

Hypothesis (H67). Expert-labelled faults that the public USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue lacks are concentrated where mapped traces stop: published fault-population studies find mapped lengths come up short because tip zones fall below the resolution of the mapping medium (about 500 m per fault in one seismic array; ≥100–200 m of DEM truncation in a rift setting). Per-pixel classification ignores that connectivity. Learning an axial orientation field (cos 2θ, sin 2θ) jointly with the fault propensity, plus a tip/junction heatmap, and decoding with orientation-aware non-maximum suppression, hysteresis and along-strike extension past mapped tips, should place emission where the catalogue is truncated rather than where it is dense.

Mechanism. (i) propensity head: gradient-boosted trees on 55 label-free physical channels; (ii) orientation head: ridge regression to cos 2θ/sin 2θ read off the visible trace mask, supervised within 3 px of visible traces; (iii) tip head: ridge regression to a Gaussian (σ = 1.5 px) heatmap at skeleton endpoints and junctions; (iv) decode: across-strike NMS (along-strike neighbours never suppress each other), Canny-style hysteresis, then a walk of up to 8 cells along the predicted strike from every tip while predicted support persists, never re-entering the catalogue; (v) nodes: 4.5 px minimum separation then calibrated greedy marginal-DTI acceptance (accept while expected new kernel credit > α·DTI, the exact first-order condition of the published metric).

Named non-fault process that could mimic the signal. Aligned ephemeral washes and fan-head channels: they are collinear, they have coherent structure-tensor orientation, they terminate in apparent “tips” at the fan apex, and they are exactly what an along-strike walk would extend. Secondary mimics: basalt flow margins, dune/deflation ridges, lithologic contacts with a resistance contrast, and anthropogenic linear features (roads, canals, field edges). The walk is required to be supported by the propensity field, which sees gravity/magnetic/conductivity channels that washes do not perturb, but the mimic is not eliminated — it is named.
