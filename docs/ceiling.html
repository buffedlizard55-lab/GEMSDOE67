Why this project's emissions park at 0.26–0.28

A root-cause analysis, computed from evidence/*.json by scripts/ceiling_analysis.py. Long form: docs/research/why-the-ceiling.md. Every number here is a holdout number unless it says leaderboard; the only organizer scores in existence are on the organizer's leaderboard.

1 · How the score is computed, in one line

The published metric is DTI = TP_w / (TP_w + 0.2·FP_w + 0.8·FN_w) with a triangular 300 m kernel. Because FN_w = |G| − TP_w exactly, it is identical to

DTI = c / (0.8 + 0.2·c + 0.2·w)

with c = TP_w/|G| the credit-weighted coverage (the share of hidden truth the emission earns) and w = FP_w/|G| the waste in units of the hidden truth count. The 0.8 term is paid for every hidden pixel whether or not it is found, so coverage is what pays and waste is a slow tax. One more dot helps exactly when its best kernel weight exceeds 0.2·DTI — the marginal bar, unit-tested in tests/test_metric.py.

The efficiency column T/S ranks these families in the exact reverse of the score column: the most efficient emission (the geometry-gated one, T/S = 0.254) scores worst, and the loosest (τ = 0.05, T/S = 0.088) never climbs either. At this ranking quality, buying coverage costs waste roughly in proportion, so the score runs along a ridge — and 0.26–0.28 is that ridge. every family that stays inside the mapped-fault neighbourhood has almost no coverage of the hidden faults (c near 0.02), so its score is bounded near 0.02; raising coverage by loosening the geometry gate raises c but also raises w, and the 0.26-0.28 band is the Pareto point those two forces reach at this head's ranking quality. It is a ceiling in information, not a bug in the writer.

The live bar is 0.4275 (xiaofanhu, read 2026-10-12). With the shipped arm's coverage, the best score physically reachable is 0.4217 — even if not one dot were wasted. with the shipped arm's coverage c = 0.3684, the absolute best achievable score at zero waste is 0.4217; the live bar 0.4275 is therefore out of reach for this coverage level, no matter how the dots are packed

So there are two levers, and they are not interchangeable: (a) coverage — a better ranking of the hidden pixels, worth ≈0.15 of headroom at the shipped coverage; (b) waste — spending mass where the model's own calibrated probability is highest, with a stopping rule taken from the marginal bar instead of a swept threshold. Lever (b) is a pure emission change on the existing head; it is registered as H67-A2 in docs/research/hypotheses.md with a ≈0.34 projection (MODEL projection under the identity above, not a score).

4 · Two caveats that cut against the pessimism

  1. w is an upper bound. The holdout's truth is the withheld catalogue. A dot on a real, coherent scarp that the catalogue never recorded is scored as pure waste by this instrument — yet the organizers' Phase-2 labels are built by expert review of Phase-1 submissions (DrivenData staff, forum thread 11527), so part of that "waste" is the signal the final round rewards.
  2. c cannot be forced. Loosening the exposure threshold to τ = 0.05 raises c to 0.508 and lowers the score to 0.2536, because w grows faster. Order matters: ranking first, packaging second.
